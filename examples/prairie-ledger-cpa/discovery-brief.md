# Discovery Brief: Prairie Ledger CPA

**PRACTICE CASE. Prairie Ledger CPA is a made-up firm. Every name and number is fictional.**

**Client:** Prairie Ledger CPA, Red Deer, Alberta
**Type and size:** Accounting firm, 14 staff (2 partners, 4 accountants, 5 bookkeepers, 2 admin, 1 office manager)
**Services:** Bookkeeping for about 90 small business clients, year-end financial statements, personal and corporate tax
**Lead source:** Claude 101 workshop, then replied to the follow-up email
**Call date:** Tuesday, November 10, 2026 (30-minute video call)
**Decision makers:** Dana and her partner Greg. Greg is more cautious about spending and "will want to know what we get for the money."
**Software in use:** Karbon, QuickBooks Online, Microsoft 365, a client portal for document uploads
**Rates (cost and billing, per role):**
- Bookkeeper: about $40/hr with everything included ($32 in wages). Bills at $75/hr. (stated)
- Partner: bills at $250/hr. Cost not given.
- Accountant: not given

---

## What they asked for

"I want AI to write our client emails. We send hundreds of them. Reminders, questions, explanations. My staff spend forever on them."

## What is actually going on

Most of those emails go to the same clients every month, chasing bank statements and receipts. Late documents push the month-end close past the 15th target. That leaves partners reviewing in a rush, and coding errors slip through until year-end. The rest of the email load is accountants writing fresh answers to the same client questions, and it gets much worse in tax season.

---

## Top problems

### Problem 1: Chasing clients for documents every month
- In their words: "Every month it's the same people. She sends the first reminder, then a second, then she phones the worst ones."
- Who and how often: All 5 bookkeepers, every month. Last month one bookkeeper had 30 of her 45 clients missing something.
- Time or cost: About 40 hrs/month across the team (estimated: "I've never actually counted")
- Knock-on effect: About half of clients close on the 20th or later, against a target of the 15th (stated)
- What they have tried: Manual email reminders, then phone calls
- Score (size / frequency / AI fit / data / risk): 4 / 5 / 5 / 4 / 5 = **23**

### Problem 2: Same client questions answered from scratch
- In their words: "Clients ask the same questions over and over. My accountants write a fresh answer every time."
- Examples given: "Why is my GST different this quarter?" "What's this entry on my statements?" "Can I deduct my truck?"
- Who and how often: Accountants, all year. "Ten times worse" from February to April.
- Time or cost: Hours not known. Asked in the follow-up email.
- What they have tried: Nothing firm-wide. Some accountants use personal ChatGPT accounts.
- Score: 3 / 5 / 5 / 3 / 3 = **19**

### Problem 3: Coding errors found at year-end instead of monthly
- In their words: "Something goes to the wrong account, and we find it at year-end. Fixing it then takes way longer."
- Who and how often: Errors happen monthly during rushed reviews. The cleanup happens once a year. Who did the cleanup is not known.
- Time or cost: 60 to 70 hrs last year-end (estimated)
- What they have tried: Partner review at month-end
- Score: 3 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 = **18**

## Build readiness

- **Software features available:**
  - Problem 1: Karbon can send automatic client reminders. Not known whether they are turned on.
  - Problem 2: Microsoft 365 (Outlook). Not known whether they use email templates.
  - Problem 3: QuickBooks Online. Not asked which review features they use.
- **Firm AI accounts:** No. Staff use personal ChatGPT accounts, possibly with client information.
- **Real examples for testing:** Not yet asked. Requested in the follow-up email.
- **Rates or hours still missing:** accountant cost and billing rate, accountant hours on client questions, who did the year-end cleanup, the partner's review hours.

## Concerns to address

- **Data privacy:** "I'm not sure what they're pasting into it. That worries me." Firm accounts and usage rules come before any testing on real records.
- **Accuracy:** ChatGPT gave "a confident wrong answer about a deduction. That spooked people." Every client-facing answer needs an accountant's review.
- **Staff fear of job loss:** One accountant asked if people are being replaced. Dana: "I'm not. I want them doing better work, not more admin."
- **Skeptical long-time staff:** The office manager (20 years at the firm) "thinks it's a fad." Two younger bookkeepers are keen.
- **Cost:** Greg wants clear numbers.

## Not now

- **Partner review time:** Hours not known, and it should drop once documents arrive on time. Track it as a result.
- **Tax season capacity:** About 15 clients turned away, about $6,750 (estimated). Driven by Problems 1 and 2.
- **Late month-end close:** A result of Problem 1. Use the share of clients closed by the 15th to measure progress.
