# Follow-up Email and Client Reply

**PRACTICE CASE. Prairie Ledger CPA is a made-up firm. The reply was written for practice.**

The Discovery Brief found missing rates and hours. Instead of guessing, the Discovery Lead drafted a short follow-up email.

---

## The follow-up email

**Subject:** A few numbers before I send the proposal

Hi Dana,

Thanks again for Tuesday's call. I heard three problems worth solving together: chasing clients for documents, answering the same client questions from scratch, and coding errors caught only at year-end.

To give you and Greg numbers you can both check, I need a few more details. Rough estimates are fine.

Most important:
1. What does an accountant cost the firm per hour with everything included, and what do you bill their time at?
2. Roughly how many hours a week does each accountant spend answering client emails? In a normal month, and February to April?
3. Who did last year's 60 to 70 hours of coding cleanup, and roughly how were the hours split?
4. Roughly how many hours a month do you spend on review work?

Also helpful:
5. Are Karbon's automatic client reminders turned on for monthly bookkeeping?
6. Could you share a few sample client emails, with names removed?

Once I have these, I'll send the full proposal within 3 business days.

Thanks,
Colette

---

## The client's reply (practice)

**Subject:** RE: A few numbers before I send the proposal

Hi Colette,

Here you go. Mostly best guesses.

1. **Accountant cost and rate:** About $45 an hour in wages, maybe $55 with everything in. We bill accountant time at $140.
2. **Client emails:** In a normal month, about 4 hours a week per accountant. February to April, closer to 10 hours a week each. There are 4 accountants.
3. **Year-end cleanup:** Roughly 40 hours were accountants, 20 hours were Greg and me, and about 5 hours were bookkeepers. We wrote off about 20 of those hours. One client also had a CRA review on GST that took an accountant about 6 extra hours.
4. **My review time:** About 15 hours a month. A senior accountant could do maybe 10 of those.
5. **Karbon reminders:** We set up Client Requests a couple of years ago, but nobody turned on the automatic reminders. Our client portal is Karbon's.
6. **Sample emails:** Yes. The most common questions are about GST, statement entries, vehicle deductions, tax instalment dates, and why they owe tax after a slow year.

Also: we're on Microsoft 365 Business Standard with no Copilot. I'd take 3 or 4 clients off the waiting list right away. Saturdays in March and April cost roughly $12,000 in overtime last season. The team can track their hours for a month.

Dana

---

## What the reply changed

- Problem 1 needs **no AI at all**. The reminders already exist in Karbon and are just turned off.
- Problems 2 and 3 can now be priced, because the accountant rate and hours are known.
- The firm agreed to track hours, so results can be measured against a real starting point.
