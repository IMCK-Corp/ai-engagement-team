# Problem 1: Karbon Automatic Reminders, Setup Checklist

**PRACTICE RUN. Based on Karbon's help pages, not tested in Prairie Ledger's Karbon account. Check each step in the account before relying on it.**

Client: Prairie Ledger CPA
Build path: Existing software (Karbon). No AI needed.
Who does it: the person with Karbon admin access, with one bookkeeper (we suggest Priya)
Time: about 4 hours, plus a 2-week trial

---

## Decisions to make first (with Dana)

| Decision | Suggested | Why |
|---|---|---|
| When requests go out | 1st business day after month-end | Clients get the request as soon as the month closes |
| Due date for documents | 5th business day of the month | Leaves 10 days to close by the 15th |
| Reminder type | Gentle: 7 days before the due date, on the due date, then daily once overdue | Karbon's built-in option. Karbon stops after the 5th reminder. |
| Who emails come from | The client's assigned bookkeeper | Clients reply to someone they know |

---

## Setup steps

1. **Find the monthly bookkeeping work template.** The Client Requests are already set up on it. Confirm the list of documents is right: bank statements, credit card statements, receipts.
2. **Turn on automatic sending.** In the template's Client Requests section, open Sending Settings. Choose to send automatically, on the date agreed above.
3. **Turn on reminders.** In the same place, choose the reminder type agreed above.
4. **Set the sender.** Choose "from" the work's assigned bookkeeper.
5. **Apply to all future months.** When Karbon asks whether to update this instance or all instances of the work, choose all.
6. **Check client emails.** Each client needs a Karbon contact with a working email address. Fix any that are missing.
7. **Set up the "who to phone" list.** Add an automation (Karbon calls these "Automators") that changes the work's status to "Call client" when all 5 reminders have been sent and the requests are still incomplete. Then save a view of all work with the status "Call client."
8. **Trial with 2 clients for 2 weeks.** Pick 2 clients who usually send documents late. Check that the requests and reminders arrive, and that the reminder emails read well.
9. **Roll out to all monthly bookkeeping clients.**

## Test (from the proposal)

| Test | How to check | Pass rate |
|---|---|---|
| Automatic requests and reminders scheduled for monthly bookkeeping clients | Count monthly bookkeeping work items with automatic sending and reminders turned on | 100% of clients |
| Trial clients receive the request and reminders | Ask the 2 trial clients, or check the work item's activity | 2 of 2 |
| "Call client" status appears after the 5th reminder | Check the trial client who has not sent documents | Works for any trial client still missing documents |
