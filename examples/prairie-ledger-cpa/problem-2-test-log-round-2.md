## What to watch

- **The new topics sit close to other questions.** Emails 7, 15 and 20 tested this: the new vehicle and instalment topics were not pulled in by mistake. Keep these three emails in every future test round.
- **14 of 14 is likely too good.** The same system wrote and graded the drafts. Expect a lower score when an accountant grades real emails. The pass rate of 12 of 14 leaves room for that.
- **Library gaps will keep appearing.** Clients will ask follow-up questions the library does not cover. The process for that: the accountant answers it, and the answer is added to the library if it comes up twice.
