## Firm Coding Rules (PRACTICE, firm must write and approve)

- Capital threshold: single purchases over **$1,500** are equipment, not an expense. [FIRM TO CONFIRM]
- Any purchase over $1,500 must have a memo that says what was bought.
- GST payments to the Receiver General or CRA go to **GST/HST Payable**.
- Transfers to an owner go to **Shareholder Loan** (corporation) or **Owner Draws** (sole proprietor).
- Possibly personal: streaming services, groceries, clothing, personal subscriptions.
